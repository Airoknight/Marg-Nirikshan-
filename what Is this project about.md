Marg Nirikshan Script
Have you ever reached a bus stop or railway station on time, yet couldn’t get on because it was already too crowded? 

This is not just a small inconvenience—it is a daily struggle for millions of people in India.

Every day, over 23 million people travel by trains and nearly 69 million use buses. This massive demand leads to severe overcrowding, causing discomfort, delays, and serious safety risks. It becomes even more difficult for elderly and differently-abled passengers. In fact, around 546 people lose their lives every day in transport-related accidents in India.

The main problem is that existing applications only provide routes and timings, but they fail to provide real-time crowd information. So people can plan their journey, but they cannot predict their actual travel experience.

To solve this problem, we propose Marg Nirikshan, an AI-powered real-time crowd density detection system.
Our system leverages existing CCTV infrastructure with edge devices like NVIDIA Jetson Nano to analyze live video feeds and estimate crowd density in real time. This information is directly delivered to a mobile app or dashboard, enabling users to check crowd levels before starting their journey and make better travel decisions.
While implementing this system, we address key challenges such as accuracy in dynamic environments, real-time processing, data privacy, and scalability across multiple locations.

For revenue, we collaborate directly with government bodies, including railways, transport departments, and smart city initiatives, by providing this as a smart infrastructure solution.
In conclusion, Marg Nirikshan doesn’t just inform—it empowers commuters to avoid congestion before it happens, transforming public transport into a safer, smarter, and more predictable system.
